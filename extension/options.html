Settings

Where files are saved

Files are saved to your browser's download location, organised as <folder>/<creator>/<format>/. To put them on another drive, change your browser's download location in its settings — or use the local bridge below.

The bridge is a small server you run yourself, so it can write anywhere you can — including external drives and network shares. Best for very large archives.

Performance

Higher values finish sooner but put more load on Meshy. Please stay considerate — 6 and 4 are sensible defaults.
