Meshy Asset Vault

Settings

Local bridge

The bridge is a small server you run yourself. It receives signed download links and writes the files to your own storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Performance

Higher values finish sooner but put more load on Meshy. Please stay considerate — 6 and 4 are sensible defaults.

Saved

This extension downloads models on your behalf using your own signed-in session. Respect each model's license and the rights of its creator, and follow Meshy's Terms of Service. See the bundled privacy policy for exactly what is stored.
